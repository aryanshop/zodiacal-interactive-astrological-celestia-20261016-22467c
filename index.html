About Zodiacal & Sky Mechanics

Zodiacal is an educational astronomical mechanics calculator designed to render precise ephemeris models entirely within your browser without external servers or proprietary APIs.

Core Features

  • Celestial Calculations: Computes mean and apparent positions of the Sun, Moon, and classical planets using astronomical algorithms based on Julian dates and obliquity of the ecliptic.
  • Dual Perspectives: Switch between a traditional 360-degree astrological natal chart wheel and an interactive sky projection map.
  • Aspect Geometries: Automatically detects major geometric alignments (Conjunction, Opposition, Trine, Square, Sextile) with exact angular tolerances.

All calculations are performed client-side using deterministic trigonometric models for educational and interactive visualization purposes.
